Chinese cabbage (bok choy) is highly nutritious, low in calories, and an excellent source of essential vitamins and minerals. It is rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, and beta-carotene, which are vital for maintaining good health, strong immune system functions, and healthy skin and vision.
